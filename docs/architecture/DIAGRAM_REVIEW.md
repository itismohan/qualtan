# Architecture Diagram Review

The detailed component diagram, `qualtan-modern-architecture.png`, captures the complete implementation-level relationship graph, including the compatibility layer, policy boundary, durable workflow, gateway, retrieval, validation gates, state, telemetry, integrations, and CI. It is intentionally wide and is best used when zoomed or viewed from its Mermaid source.

The primary delivery visual is `qualtan-architecture-overview.png`. It is a 3120×1612 PNG with a readable top-level control-plane structure. It clearly separates sources, entry points, governed control plane, intelligence and retrieval, deterministic assurance, durable state and telemetry, external systems, and delivery outputs. It is suitable for architecture reviews and production-readiness documentation.

Both diagrams are deterministic Mermaid sources, so labels and edges remain editable and auditable.
